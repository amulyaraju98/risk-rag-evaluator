# Merchant Onboarding & KYC/KYB Procedures

**Company:** ApexPay  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Compliance Team  

## What This Document Covers
How to check if a new merchant is real and trustworthy before letting them transact on NexusPay.

---

## Merchant Risk Categories
### 1. Low Risk – Easy Check 
Examples → Local retail, salons, cafés  
Required Docs:
- Personal ID (Passport/Driving License)  
- Proof of Address (electricity bill or bank statement)  
- Business license (if applicable)  
Time → 1–2 days   Method → Automated + Manual

### 2. Medium Risk – Standard Check 
Examples → Online store, freelancer, SaaS startup  
Required Docs:
- All Low Risk documents  
- 3‑month bank statement  
- Tax ID (PAN/TIN/VAT)  
- Ownership structure details  
Time → 3–5 days   Method → Manual + third‑party verification

### 3. High Risk – Enhanced Check 
Examples → Crypto, gambling, FX trading  
Required Docs:
- Everything from Medium Risk  
- Source‑of‑funds proof  
- Beneficial ownership declaration  
- Compliance officer approval  
Time → 7–10 days   Method → Enhanced Due Diligence

---

## Verification Process (6 Steps)
1. Receive application + documents  
2. Check for completeness  
3. Validate authenticity (document fraud scan)  
4. Cross‑check against sanctions/PEP lists (OFAC/FATF)  
5. Assess business model risk and jurisdiction  
6. Decision → Approve/Reject + record justification  

---

## Rejection Reasons
- Fake/missing documents  
- Sanctions or PEP match  
- High‑risk jurisdiction (FATF grey/black list)  
- Opaque ownership chains  
- Failed background check  

---

## Appeal Procedure
Rejected merchants may re‑apply within 30 days after fixing deficiencies.  
All records kept for 5 years per RBI guidelines.
