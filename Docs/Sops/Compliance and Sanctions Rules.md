# SOP 003: AML/KYC Compliance & Sanctions Screening

**Company:** ApexPay  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Compliance & Risk Team  
**Regulatory Frameworks:** FATF Guidelines, RBI Master Direction (KYC 2023), PSD2 (EU)

---

## Objective
To prevent money laundering and terrorist financing by screening all merchants against sanctions lists and conducting Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD).

---

## Step 1 – PEP (Politically Exposed Persons)
**Definition:** Individuals holding public office or close associates/family.  
**Procedure:**  
1. Screen merchant + beneficial owners against PEP database.  
2. If match → classify High Risk and perform EDD.  
3. Record source of wealth and funds.  
**Risk Levels:** Direct PEP 🔴  |  Family Member 🟠  |  Associate 🟡  

---

## Step 2 – Sanctions List Checks 🚫
Lists used: OFAC (US), UN, EU, FATF, RBI.  
**Actions:**  
- Exact/partial name match = immediate freeze of account.  
- Report within 24 h to FIU‑IND or relevant body.  
- No activity until official clearance.  

---

## Step 3 – High‑Risk Jurisdiction Review
| Category | Examples | Action |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Low Risk  ✅ | US, UK, Japan | Standard KYC |
| Medium ⚠️ | India, UAE, Singapore | Light EDD |
| High 🟠 | Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Turkey | Full EDD |
| Critical 🔴 | Iran, N. Korea | Block + Report |

---

## Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
Triggered when: PEP found / High‑Risk Country / Suspicious Transaction.  
Includes:  
- Interview + business model review  
- Source‑of‑funds evidence  
- Ownership verification  
- Tax returns + bank statements  
- Third‑party background check  
Time: 10–20 business days.  

---

## Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR)
File SAR if you see:  
- Sanctioned entity match  
- Hidden beneficial owners  
- Unexplained large flow of funds  
- Structuring to avoid limits  
**Report To:** FIU‑IND (India), FATF, FinCEN, EASA (EU).  
Deadline: 24 h within detection.  

---

## Risk Scoring Matrix 
| Level | Description | Action |
|-------|--------------|--------|
| 1 – Green ✅ | FATF compliant, transparent ownership | Standard KYC |
| 2 – Yellow ⚠️ | Minor issues, mid‑risk country | Light EDD |
| 3 – Orange 🟠 | PEP / opaque ownership | Full EDD + Quarterly review |
| 4 – Red 🔴 | Sanctioned / AML red flags | Suspend + File SAR |

---

## Ongoing Monitoring 
Re‑screen merchants based on risk level:   
- Low → Annually  |  Medium → Quarterly  |  High → Monthly  
Checks → new sanctions, adverse news, ownership changes.

---

## Regulatory Highlights
- RBI: KYC within 24 h of onboarding; records stored 5 yrs.  
- FATF: Follow 40 + 9 recommendations.  
- PSD2: Strong Customer Authentication + data protection.
